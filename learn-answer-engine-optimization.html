What is answer engine optimization?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is making your content clear and factual enough that AI answer engines, like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, cite your business when people ask.

Get a free SEO proposal All guides
In short

The plain definition

AEO is SEO for AI answers: structuring pages so a machine can read them, trust them and quote them, with a link back to you.

How it works

Answer engines read structured, factual pages and quote the ones that answer a question directly, come from a real source and can be verified.

In practice

How AEO works in practice

Lead with the direct answer, so an engine can lift it cleanly.

Earn trust

Common mistakes

Common mistakes

Vague claims

"Best in town" with nothing to back it is not citable.

No sources

Facts with no source are hard for an engine to trust.

Hiding the answer

Burying the answer under intros means it won't get quoted.

Invented stats

Made-up numbers break trust and can be penalised.

FAQ

Common questions

What is AEO?
Making your content clear and factual enough that AI answer engines cite your business.
Is AEO different from SEO?
It overlaps, but AEO optimises for being quoted in AI answers, not just ranking in links.
Which engines matter?
ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini are the main ones today.
How do I get cited?
Answer the question directly, back it with verifiable facts and schema, and show a real author.
