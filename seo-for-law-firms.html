FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO for a law firm cost?
There is no single price for every firm. Cost depends on the pages and tasks included, such as content edits, technical checks and local information. We send a scoped proposal after reviewing one of your existing pages. The current plans and prices are on our pricing page.
How long does it take to see changes?
Indexing and ranking move on Google's timeline, not a fixed schedule. We start with one page, show what changed in the monthly report, and build from there. We never promise a date for a ranking.
Can you guarantee a first-page ranking or more cases?
No. No provider can honestly guarantee a ranking position or a number of cases. We scope specific work and report its effect; outcomes depend on many factors outside any provider's control.
What should I provide before you edit a practice-area page?
The facts for that practice area, your firm's approved name, locations, hours and contact details, and the person who signs off public claims. We write from what the firm verifies.
Should we create a separate page for every service and location?
Only where the firm genuinely serves that area or offers that service. We build pages that answer a real inquiry, not thin city-swapped copies, which Google treats as spam.
How do we know a consultation form is working?
We agree on what counts as an inquiry up front, a button click, a received message and a confirmed consultation are different events, and the monthly report tracks the one you chose.
