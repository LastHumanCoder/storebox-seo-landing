What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work that helps a business show up when nearby customers search for what it sells, on Google Search, Maps and in AI answers.

Get a free SEO proposal All guides
In short

The plain definition

It is everything that tells Google where you are, what you do and that you can be trusted, so you appear for "near me" and city searches.

How it works

Google reads your website, your Google Business Profile and mentions of your business across the web, then matches them to a searcher's location and intent.

In practice

How local SEO works in practice

Your profile & location

Google Business Profile, address, hours and categories tell Google where and what you are.

Trust signals

Consistent details, genuine reviews and mentions build the trust Google ranks on.

Pages that match intent

A clear page for each real location or service, matched to what people search.

Common mistakes

Common mistakes

Fake or shared addresses

Listing areas you don't serve, which Google treats as spam.

Inconsistent details

A different phone or address across listings confuses Google.

Ignoring reviews

Reviews are a trust signal; not asking leaves ranking on the table.

Thin city-swapped pages

One template with the city swapped adds no value and can be penalised.

FAQ

Common questions

What is local SEO?
The work that helps a business appear when nearby customers search, on Search, Maps and in AI answers.
How is it different from normal SEO?
Local SEO adds location signals, your Google Business Profile and reviews on top of normal on-page and content work.
How long does local SEO take?
It varies; profile and consistency fixes can help within weeks, while ranking moves on Google's timeline.
Do reviews affect local ranking?
Genuine reviews are a trust signal and support local ranking; fake reviews are against the rules.
